01 ABOUT
A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Just a student who enjoys working with data.

Still learning, still figuring things out.

I am an Information Systems student at BINUS University with an interest in Data Analysis, Business Analysis, and Business Intelligence.

When I first chose the Business Intelligence track, I did not really know what to expect. As I explored different subjects, I started to enjoy working with data, especially when it involved Excel, dashboards, and data visualization.

From there, I started exploring tools such as Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Python, and Oracle. I enjoy the process of taking something that initially looks like a lot of numbers and turning it into information that is easier to understand.

Outside academics, I have also been active in HIMSISFO BINUS University. Being involved in different divisions and events pushed me outside my comfort zone. I started from an activist role, later became a Vice Coordinator, and eventually took the role of Project Manager for one of the organization's programs.

I am still exploring where I want to go next, but right now I am most interested in opportunities where I can combine data, business, and technology.

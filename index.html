ABOUT ME

Just a student who enjoys working with data.

I started exploring Information Systems without really knowing which part of it I would enjoy the most. As I went through different courses, I gradually found myself more interested in the parts that involved data, visualization, and figuring out what the numbers were actually telling me.

Business Intelligence became one of the areas that caught my attention. I enjoy working with tools such as Excel, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Python, and Oracle while learning how data can be organized and presented in a way that is easier to understand.

Outside the classroom, my involvement in HIMSISFO has also shaped how I work with other people. From supporting events and handling equipment to becoming a Vice Coordinator and Project Manager, those experiences taught me how important communication, teamwork, and responsibility are.

Right now, I'm mainly interested in opportunities related to Data Analysis, with Business Analysis and Business Intelligence as areas I would also like to explore further.

01 · About me

Learning data,
building experience.

I’m an Information Systems student at BINUS University, currently focusing on Business Intelligence.

At first, I chose BI without really knowing what it was about. After a semester, I started enjoying the process — especially working with data, making visualizations, and building dashboards that make information easier to understand. I’ve worked on dashboards using Excel, Tableau, and Oracle, and I’ve also written research papers and scientific articles as part of my learning process.

Outside of academics, I’m active in HIMSISFO. I started out more introverted, but over time I became more comfortable speaking, working with different people, and taking responsibility. I was trusted as a Vice Coordinator and later a Project Manager for an event.

Right now, I’m interested in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, and Business Analysis. I’m still learning, building projects, and figuring out which path fits me best.

02
ABOUT ME
✦

Just a student who enjoys working with data.

I'm currently studying Information Systems at Bina Nusantara University. When I first chose the Business Intelligence track, I honestly didn't know much about what Business Intelligence actually involved.

As I went through my first semester, I started to realize that I enjoyed the part where we worked with data. I liked organizing information, looking at numbers, creating dashboards, and trying to understand what the data was actually telling us.

That interest gradually led me to explore tools such as Excel, Tableau, Power BI, SQL, Python, and Oracle Database. Most of what I learned came from classes, assignments, small projects, and simply trying things out when I was curious about something.

Outside academics, I've also spent a lot of time being involved in HIMSISFO. At first, I was more comfortable staying behind the scenes and focusing on my own tasks. Being part of different events slowly pushed me to communicate more, work with different people, and take responsibility for things that could affect the whole team.

Over time, I became more comfortable taking on bigger responsibilities, from working in divisions to becoming a Vice Coordinator and later a Project Manager. Those experiences taught me things that I probably wouldn't have learned just from a classroom.

Right now, I'm interested in opportunities related to Data Analysis, Business Analysis, and Business Intelligence. I'm still learning, but I enjoy the process of figuring things out and getting better at what I do.
